# MASTER CONTEXT

## Project Identity
PUB ECOM = Central Operator / managed commerce and marketplace foundation.
Mentorado = Sócio Investidor / authenticated platform participant.
Store = commercial sales boundary and storefront.
Customer = end customer.
Supplier = upstream product provider managed by PUB ECOM.
Affiliate = performance partner paid by percentage of attributed sales.
Influencer = performance creator paid from the net profit generated by attributed sales.

## Operating Model
PUB ECOM is the central operator of the commerce chain.

Customer purchase flow:
1. Customer buys through a Store.
2. The Order identifies the selling Store, product and Supplier.
3. PUB ECOM operationally purchases the product from the Supplier.
4. Supplier fulfills the product to the end customer's shipping address.
5. PUB ECOM controls the operational, financial, attribution and fulfillment lifecycle.

The Store does not need to negotiate with suppliers or operate physical inventory.

## Growth / Acquisition Model
PUB ECOM has a unified acquisition and attribution layer.

### Core funnel
1. `page_view`
2. `add_to_cart`
3. `add_payment_info`
4. `purchase`

Every event should preserve, where available:
- event_id
- session_id
- customer_id or anonymous identity
- store_id
- product_id
- affiliate_id
- influencer_id
- campaign_id
- UTM source/medium/campaign/content/term
- landing page
- timestamp

### Remarketing levels
- Level 1: Page View without purchase.
- Level 2: Add to Cart without purchase.
- Level 3: Add Payment Info without purchase.
- Level 4: Purchase, used for retention, cross-sell, upsell and repurchase rather than abandonment recovery.

Each funnel level must support custom audiences, configurable retention windows and purchase exclusions.

## Affiliate Model
Affiliates are performance partners.

- No fixed payment for work.
- Affiliate generates an attributed sale.
- Affiliate receives a configurable percentage of the attributed sale according to the active commission rule.
- Affiliate has a dedicated dashboard with clicks, sessions, carts, checkouts, sales and commissions.
- Affiliate attribution must be connected to the same session/order model used by ads and influencers.

## Influencer / Creator Model
Influencers are performance creators with a higher economic share than standard affiliates because they contribute distribution through their audience and content production.

- No fixed payment for work in the baseline model.
- Influencer receives 50% of the net profit generated by attributed sales, subject to the active commercial agreement.
- Net profit must be calculated from the actual transaction economics, not from gross sales alone.

Baseline calculation:
`Gross Sale - Product Cost - Shipping Cost - Payment Fees - Refunds - Discounts - Other Deductible Costs = Net Profit`

`Influencer Earnings = Net Profit × Influencer Share`

The default product requirement is 50%, but the system must model the share as configurable rather than hard-coded.

Influencer attribution supports:
- creator links;
- creator codes;
- clicks;
- sessions;
- carts;
- checkouts;
- purchases;
- generated revenue;
- generated net profit;
- creator earnings.

## Live Shop
MASTER should provide real-time operational visibility similar to modern commerce admin platforms.

Key live metrics:
- visitors online;
- product views;
- open carts;
- active checkouts;
- simultaneous sales;
- revenue now/today;
- live order feed;
- store attribution.

The implementation should use an event-driven real-time channel such as WebSocket or SSE rather than relying exclusively on page refreshes.

## Central Finance
MASTER finance starts with store-level performance and rolls up to the platform.

Core views:
- stores registered;
- total revenue;
- revenue per store;
- orders per store;
- average order value;
- product cost;
- shipping cost;
- payment fees;
- refunds/discounts;
- net operating result;
- affiliate commissions;
- influencer profit shares;
- payable/available balances.

Store ranking and financial reporting must be separate concerns from accounting settlement, while both use the same transaction source of truth.

## Rankings / Incentives
### Store ranking
Monthly store performance ranking with configurable scoring.
Baseline dimensions:
- revenue;
- orders;
- conversion;
- growth versus prior period;
- operational quality.

### Affiliate ranking
Performance by attributed revenue, orders and commission generation.

### Influencer ranking
Performance by attributed revenue, net profit generated and creator earnings.

### Awards / Bonuses
MASTER should support configurable monthly awards, targets, campaigns and bonus rules.

## SEO / Product Discovery
Every cataloged product should be eligible for an indexable product page with:
- canonical URL;
- title and meta description;
- H1 and structured product content;
- product structured data;
- price and availability data;
- Open Graph metadata;
- sitemap inclusion.

Catalog → normalized product → SEO page → sitemap/feed → search discovery is a core acquisition path.

SEO must prioritize useful, indexable product/category content and must not generate empty or manipulative pages solely for search-engine coverage.

## Checkout / Payments
Checkout is transparent and provider-agnostic.

Required architecture:
- preserve attribution and UTM context through checkout;
- support gateway abstraction;
- process payment status through server-side events/webhooks;
- reconcile payment transaction with Order and Finance;
- emit `purchase` only after authoritative payment confirmation according to the payment state machine.

## Data / Attribution Principle
A purchase is not merely an `order = paid` record.
The authoritative purchase event must feed:
- order state;
- financial ledger;
- attribution;
- affiliate commission;
- influencer profit share;
- store performance;
- rankings;
- fulfillment;
- post-purchase audience membership;
- analytics.

## Checkout Foundation
- Checkout transaction boundaries are encapsulated in PostgreSQL RPCs.
- Inventory reservation is handled safely at the database level during checkout creation.
- Checkout expiration has an explicit lifecycle.
- Guest/customer context is resolved securely.
- SECURITY DEFINER RPCs enforce the relevant database boundary.

## Security
- RLS is the final database enforcement layer.
- Application authorization is a pre-check, not a replacement for RLS.
- SERVICE_ROLE is server-only.
- Anonymous access is explicitly granted only where required.
- External provider secrets are never exposed to browser code.
- Customer personal data must be minimized in partner/provider surfaces.
- Attribution identifiers must not expose unnecessary PII.
